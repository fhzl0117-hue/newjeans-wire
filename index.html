SEOUL — SEPTEMBER 2026

Four, After
the Pause.

On July 22, 2026 — their fourth debut anniversary — NewJeans posted "2026 Summer of NewJeans," an anniversary film and individual member clips featuring Minji, Hanni, Haerin, and Hyein. It was the group's first joint public content in roughly 16 months, following a long-running contract dispute with agency ADOR that had kept them off stages and out of new releases since mid-2024. No comeback single, EP, or release date has been officially confirmed as of this writing — this page tracks what's been announced, not what fans expect. This is a working build of how NewJeans Wire tracks it all for English-speaking Bunnies — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

A note on where things stand: in November 2024, members said they intended to end their exclusive contract with ADOR, a HYBE label, following the dismissal of founding CEO Min Hee-jin. A March 2025 Seoul court injunction barred the group's independent activity under a rebranded "NJZ" name pending the case, and in October 2025 the Seoul Central District Court ruled that the ADOR contract remained valid, rejecting the termination claim. ADOR separately stated in December 2025 that it had ended Danielle's membership in the group. This page treats the group's current, confirmed lineup as four members and won't characterize the dispute's outcome, Danielle's status, or any next steps beyond what's been reported by named outlets — linked below.

Desk 01 · News

The dispute, in the order it happened.

Every dispatch below links back to its original source — this desk summarizes, it doesn't replace, and it won't speculate past what's been reported.

SEOUL — SEP 7

Haerin makes her first public appearance in about a year and a half

Haerin attended a beauty brand photocall at Lotte Department Store's Jamsil branch in Seoul on September 7, wearing a black tweed dress under a white cardigan. She introduced herself to the room as "NewJeans' Haerin" and thanked fans with a smile. It was her first solo public appearance since the group's contract dispute with ADOR began, and comes after she and Hyein returned to the label in November 2025 and Hanni followed in December — while Danielle's contract has been terminated and Minji's situation remains under discussion.

Summarized from allkpop by NewJeans Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

SEOUL — NOV 2024

Members declare intent to terminate their ADOR contract

Following the removal of founding CEO Min Hee-jin earlier in 2024, NewJeans' members announced they intended to end their exclusive contract with ADOR, a HYBE-owned label, arguing the label had breached its obligations. ADOR disputed the claim, setting up a legal fight that would keep the group off new music and stages for well over a year.

Summarized from Rolling Stone and Music Business Worldwide by NewJeans Wire staff.

Read background ↗

HONG KONG — MAR 23

Group performs under "NJZ" branding at ComplexCon, then pauses

Days after a Seoul court granted ADOR an injunction barring independent activity, the group performed at ComplexCon Hong Kong under an unofficial "NJZ" rebrand — playing covers and an unreleased song, "Pit Stop," while avoiding their NewJeans back catalog. Members closed the set by announcing a hiatus to the crowd of roughly 11,000.

Summarized from TIME by NewJeans Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

SEOUL — OCT 30

Court rules ADOR's contract with the group remains valid

The Seoul Central District Court found it "difficult to conclude that ADOR breached its exclusive contract solely on the grounds of Min Hee-jin's dismissal," rejecting the members' November 2024 termination claim. The ruling meant the group's exclusive agreement with ADOR stayed in force, and the "NJZ" rebrand could not proceed as an independent activity.

Summarized from Music Business Worldwide by NewJeans Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

SEOUL — DEC 2025

ADOR ends Danielle's membership in the group

ADOR stated it had "determined that it would be difficult to continue together as a NewJeans member and an ADOR artist" regarding member Danielle, ending her place in the group. The label did not announce a replacement, and the group's subsequent public content has featured its remaining four members.

Summarized from PhilSTAR Life by NewJeans Wire staff.

Read the original source ↗

SEOUL — JUL 22

"2026 Summer of NewJeans" marks the group's public return

At midnight on their fourth debut anniversary, NewJeans posted an anniversary film and individual clips for Minji, Hanni, Haerin, and Hyein across official channels — described by outlets as the group's first coordinated public content in about 16 months, though not a formal comeback announcement. No release date for new music has been confirmed since.

Summarized from PhilSTAR Life and The FADER by NewJeans Wire staff.

Desk 02 · Review

Revisiting the catalogue, track by track.

Text only, always — no reposted photos or video frames on this desk. Ratings and pull quotes are sourced from named outside critics, not written in-house. With no new release to cover, this desk looks back at where it all started.

9.25 / 10THE BIAS LIST
"They genuinely have some of the best vocal direction/tones n above all else ARTISTRY I've ever heard in kpop."

New Jeans (2022): the debut EP that broke first-week sales records for a K-pop girl group. In a May 2026 retrospective rating every NewJeans single, The Bias List scored debut lead track "Attention" at 9.25/10 — among the group's highest-ever marks, alongside perfect 10s later given to "Hype Boy," "Super Shy," and "Ditto." The debut EP itself pulled in over 444,000 pre-orders, sold more than 262,800 first-day copies (breaking a record set months earlier by labelmate Le Sserafim), and landed at #46 on Rolling Stone's 100 Best Albums of 2022.

It's the era this page keeps circling back to while waiting on what comes next — a reminder of why the wait has drawn so much attention in the first place.
